# 🚀 Astro HR

### Smart HR & Attendance Management Software

**Astro HR** is a modern Human Resource Management solution designed to simplify employee administration, attendance tracking, and daily HR operations.

Developed by **[Code Astro](https://www.codeastro.dev)**, Astro HR helps businesses streamline workforce management, organize employee records, and improve operational efficiency.

## Key Features

- 👥 **Employee Management** — Maintain organized employee profiles and records.
- ⏱️ **Attendance Tracking** — Monitor employee attendance and working hours.
- 🕒 **Shift Management** — Organize employee shifts and work schedules.
- 🏖️ **Leave Management** — Manage leave requests and approvals.
- 📊 **HR Reports** — Review attendance summaries and workforce information.
- 💰 **Payroll Support** — Support payroll-related workflows based on configured features.
- 🖥️ **Device Integration** — Support compatible biometric attendance devices, such as ZKTeco, where implemented.
- 🔐 **Access Control** — Protect HR information through appropriate permissions and security controls.

*Features and integrations depend on the deployed version and configuration.*

## Why Astro HR?

- Reduce manual HR administration.
- Improve attendance record accuracy.
- Simplify workforce management.
- Save time on routine HR tasks.
- Support data-driven HR decisions.
